About Toolnuri

Toolnuri is a collection of free calculators and conversion tools available without account registration.

Most calculations run in your browser. Currency results use a dated external reference rate; financial and safety-related results are informational, not professional advice.

Tools available

Area, length and temperature converters, a scientific calculator, loan repayment comparisons, a currency converter and image/PDF conversion tools.

How to use results responsibly

Each tool explains its assumptions, sample calculations and limitations. Double-check units, inputs and rate dates when a result seems unexpected.
