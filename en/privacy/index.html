Privacy information

Toolnuri does not provide account registration and does not require your name or contact details to run standard calculators.

Standard calculators operate in your browser. Image and PDF conversion is implemented in-browser without an upload endpoint operated by Toolnuri, although PDF-related code may load libraries from an external CDN.

Third parties and technical data

Hosting/security provider Cloudflare and advertising provider Google AdSense may process technical connection data and cookies. Google’s policies apply to its advertising services. The currency calculator requests the selected currency pair from the Frankfurter API.

Questions and updates

Use the contact/report page for privacy inquiries. This notice should be updated if the site’s actual data processing changes.

See the Google Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
