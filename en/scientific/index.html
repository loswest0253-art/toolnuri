How to use

Trigonometric functions support DEG (degrees) and RAD (radians). log is the base-10 logarithm; ln is the natural logarithm.

Parentheses and arithmetic are supported. Explicitly enter multiplication as 2×π . Invalid calculations, such as division by zero, return an error.

This general-purpose real-number calculator is not a substitute for safety-critical calculations.
